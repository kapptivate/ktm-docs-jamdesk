The mini cellular robot has no video output. Plugging a screen into it gets you nowhere: the only way in is the DB9 socket on its back panel. Connect a serial-to-USB adapter there and open a terminal application on your computer. You get the same console every other robot shows on a monitor.

<Note>
  This model belongs to the previous generation of Kapptivate hardware and is being replaced. Its back panel gives it away: a DB9 socket where the other models carry a video output. Those take a screen and a keyboard instead.
</Note>

## What you need

- The robot, powered on and within reach. A serial line is a cable, so you can't do this from your desk.
- A serial-to-USB adapter: DB9 on the robot side, USB-A on the computer side.
- The adapter's driver, installed on your computer. Ask support for the download link that matches your adapter model.
- A serial terminal application. The steps below use **CoolTerm**, which is free and runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- The robot's credentials, the ones support gave you with the robot.

<Frame caption="The serial-to-USB adapter">
  ![A serial-to-USB adapter, with a DB9 connector at one end and a USB-A plug at the other](/images/hardware/headless-login-02.webp)
</Frame>

## Open the console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the adapter">
    Plug the DB9 end into the serial socket on the back panel of the robot, and the USB end into your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the port">
    Open CoolTerm and list the available ports. Plugging the adapter in added one: `/dev/tty.usbserial-*` on macOS, a `COM` number on Windows, and `/dev/ttyUSB0` on Linux. Select it. No new port means the driver isn't installed, or the adapter is plugged into a dead USB port.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the port parameters">
    | Parameter | Value |
    |-----------|-------|
    | Baud rate | 115200 |
    | Data bits | 8 |
    | Parity | None |
    | Stop bits | 1 |
    | Flow control | None |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    The window stays black.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Enter">
    The robot's login prompt appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Enter the robot's credentials. You land in a shell.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A black window before you press Enter is the normal state, not a failure: a serial line shows nothing until something travels over it.
</Note>

## Check that the console answers

Run a command that proves the shell is alive:

```bash
hostname
```

The robot returns its name. From there the console behaves like any other, starting with [identifying the RJ45 port](/hardware/rj45-port) that carries the static IP.

## When nothing shows up

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| A black window after you press Enter | Check that the robot is powered on, then that flow control is set to None. |
| Garbled characters | The baud rate is wrong. Set it back to 115200. |
| No serial port in the list | The driver isn't installed, or the adapter is plugged into a dead USB port. Unplug it, plug it back in, and look at the port list again. |
