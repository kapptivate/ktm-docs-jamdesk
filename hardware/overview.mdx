Kapptivate runs your tests from robots deployed in your own network: web, smartphone, and cellular robots holding real SIM cards. This section covers installing one, keeping it running, and finding the fault when it stops answering.

A robot is the hardware: box, power cable, antennas, SIM cards, Ethernet ports. The agent is the software running on it, and the agent is what you manage from the platform. That split runs through the pages below: you power a robot off, you restart an agent.

The installation pages cover the latest robot generation; contact support before installing an older one. Maintenance and troubleshooting cover both generations.
