---
title: Display mode
description: Switch the interface between light, dark, and your system setting.
---

Kapptivate opens in light. If you watch monitors late, sit next to a wall display, or simply prefer a dark desktop, you can switch the whole interface to dark from your account preferences.

## Switch the interface to dark

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the sidebar and select **Settings**
2. Open **Preferences** under My account
3. In the anchor menu on the left, click **Display mode**
4. Select **Dark**

The interface changes as you click. There is nothing to save here: the **Save changes** button covers the date format and the language on that page, not the display mode.

## The three options

| Option | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | The default. A light interface, whatever your computer is set to. |
| **Dark** | A dark interface, whatever your computer is set to. |
| **Auto** | Kapptivate follows your operating system. |

Auto reads the appearance setting of your system: **System Settings > Appearance** on macOS, **Settings > Personalization > Colors** on Windows. Switch your system to dark while Kapptivate is open and the interface follows right away, without a reload.

## Your choice stays on this browser

The display mode is stored in the browser you set it in, not on your Kapptivate account. Open the application on another computer, in another browser, or in a private window, and it starts in light again. Set it once in each browser you work from. Clearing the site's browsing data also resets it.

<Tip>
Pick **Auto** in each browser you use, and your operating system decides everywhere, once and for all.
</Tip>

## Wall displays keep their own switch

[Status Details](/monitoring/status-details) has a TV mode with its own day/night switch in the top bar. That switch wins over your display mode for as long as the page stays in TV mode, so a light wall display and a dark interface for yourself work together without conflict. The choice travels in the page URL, so a link pinned on a screen reopens with the theme it was saved with. Leave TV mode and the page goes back to your display mode.

Your other personal settings, the date and time format and the interface language, live on the same page: see [Preferences](/administration/account/preferences).
